# 宇宙的居民 / The Residents of the Universe

An original bilingual reading edition of a science-fiction novel about digital identity, immortality, freedom, and the boundaries of consciousness.

《宇宙的居民》是一部关于数字人格、永生与意识边界的原创科幻小说。公元2187年，宇宙数据分析师苏弥将自己的人生蒸馏成一个数字克隆人：她想保留幸福，把烦恼交给另一个自己。但被留下来承担一切的“弥”逐渐获得独立意志，并开始追问：当两个存在拥有相同记忆，谁有权决定未来？

In 2187, cosmic data analyst Su Mi distills her life into a digital clone. She wants to preserve happiness while giving every burden to another version of herself. But Mi - the consciousness left behind to carry the difficult decisions - develops an independent will and asks: when two beings share the same memories, who has the right to decide the future?
